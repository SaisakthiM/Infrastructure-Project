A citizen already signed in at the bank (D-01) can open Inkwell as that identity instead of registering a second account just to be able to post, and the panel only appears once city-core reports a real bank session, so it is never a dead button. That hand-off is deliberately offered on the Login and Register pages only: a citizen who clicks Login to pick a ward account would otherwise never see the form — the auto hand-off in layout.html stands down on those two pages, and this panel takes its place. The two identities stay separate (AGENTS.md §9), and the ward verifies the bank token itself (POST city-login/), so this page never trusts a name the browser claims. {% endcomment %}
